A CAREER ACROSS INFORMATION SYSTEMS

From formal models to human experience.

Paolo Paolini’s work links two questions: how information should be structured, and how people experience it. His research spans relational databases, hypertext and web application design, multimedia communication, cultural heritage, and education.

That breadth gives the work a consistent thread: making complex information easier to navigate, interpret, and use.

RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Four fields, connected by a common question.

How should information be structured, explored, and understood? The work approaches that question through data, the web, culture, and learning.

MODEL

Database theory & information models

Work on relational databases, consistent views, and update semantics explores how information can be represented precisely and how changes should be handled.

DATABASE THEORY
NAVIGATE

Hypertext, hypermedia & web engineering

Conceptual models make content, links, and navigation explicit before implementation. This line of work includes the Hypertext Design Model (HDM) and model-driven web applications.
