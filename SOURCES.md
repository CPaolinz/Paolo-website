# Research and source notes

This microsite is a curated academic profile, not an official university page or a complete CV. Factual claims were checked against the sources below. Items taken from the CV text supplied by the site owner are identified where independently verifying them was not possible.

## Institutional profiles and current research context

- **Politecnico di Milano, DEIB profile** — https://www.deib.polimi.it/eng/people/details/60294
  - Lists Paolo Paolini as a retired professor in Computer Science and Engineering; the research line is “Data, web, and society.”
  - Describes interests in multimedia application requirements and design, collaborative e-learning applications, technology-mediated communication, and cultural heritage.
  - Reports more than 70 papers on the institutional page. The website deliberately avoids a personal publication-count claim because other sources use different scopes/counts.
- **Politecnico di Milano, DOL faculty page** — https://www.dol.polimi.it/chi-siamo/i-docenti/
  - States he was Full Professor of Computer Science at Politecnico di Milano until 2016; confirms education in Physics at the University of Milan and Master's/Ph.D. in Computer Science at UCLA.
  - Lists research across databases, hypertext, multimedia applications, usability, web design, accessibility, cultural heritage, and education/e-learning. It also mentions more recent work on educational chatbots and teaching paradigms.
- **HOC-LAB official website** — https://www.hoc.polimi.it/en/home/
  - Identifies Paolo Paolini as Senior Scientific Advisor, Computer Science.
  - Describes HOC-LAB as a multidisciplinary multimedia communication laboratory founded in 1997, with education as its main application field.
  - Its current site describes PoliCultura, EVOLI, and other educational initiatives. Lab-wide impact counts belong to HOC-LAB, not to Paolo personally, and are not presented as personal achievements here.
- **Portrait** — The site uses the local portrait crop supplied by the site owner in `assets/paolo-paolini-profile.jpg`. The official HOC-LAB hosted image was not downloaded during this build, so no external hotlinked image is required by the current page.
- **HOC-LAB research** — https://www.hoc.polimi.it/en/research/
  - Summarises current work on augmented-reality experiences, video annotation, educational chatbots, multimedia storytelling, and technology-supported learning.
- **Franca Garzotto's Politecnico profile** — https://garzotto.faculty.polimi.it/
  - Provides historical context for the HDM model and early hypertext/hypermedia design work involving Paolo Paolini, Franca Garzotto, and Daniel Schwabe.
